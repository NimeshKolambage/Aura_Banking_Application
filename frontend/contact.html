Frequently Asked Questions

How do I open an account?

Opening an account is simple! Click "Get Started" and follow our quick registration process. You'll need an email address and valid ID.

Is my money secure?

Yes! All accounts are FDIC insured up to $250,000 and protected with bank-level encryption and multi-factor authentication.

Are there any fees?

No! Aura charges zero monthly fees, zero overdraft fees, and zero account maintenance fees. We believe in transparent banking.
